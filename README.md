## The case

At 02:17 AM, a receiver inside an apartment activates even though it has no power. A message arrives through the owner's own phone number. The workstation is still on, the network connection is gone, and someone appears to have been using the device before its owner touched it.

A voice recording contains a familiar voice — but the person who recognizes it insists the speaker was somewhere else.

The investigation leads to **Node 09**, a hidden archive that appears to store voices before their owners remember speaking. Its access log contains an impossible timestamp, Mara Voss knows more than she admits, and the final signal seems to originate from the investigator's own device.

The central question is not simply who sent the signal. It is whether the signal was received at all — or whether it was sent from a version of events that has not happened yet.

BLACK FILES is a fictional, interactive psychological-thriller investigation. Reconstruct the case by exploring simulated device interfaces, decrypting messages, recovering files, connecting evidence and making consequential decisions.

## How to play

The investigation is presented through four connected sections:

- **DESKTOP** — explore the recovered workstation, files, system notices and timestamps.
- **PHONE** — inspect encrypted messages and interactive incoming-call sequences involving Unknown, Mara and the system.
- **EVIDENCE** — collect nine core clues and connect them to confirm three theories.
- **CASE FILE** — review the investigation timeline, solve two forensic puzzles and make decisions that shape the ending.

## The three chapters

### CHAPTER 01 — THE MISSING SIGNAL
The workstation shows activity at 02:17 AM. A message from an unknown relay address says the receiver woke up without power and that the message was routed through the investigator's own number. Mara claims she heard the investigator's voice on a recording, even though the investigator was not there.

### CHAPTER 02 — THE TRACE
The signal has an apparent destination. A hidden relay has answered, but part of its route has been erased. The recovered route points from the local device through **Node 09** to the **Old Observatory**.

### CHAPTER 03 — THE VOICE
The investigation reaches a hidden partition on the local device. A voiceprint matches the device owner, a mirror protocol describes a second instance, and the origin data points back to the current device.

## Investigation features

- **9 core clues** to recover and examine.
- **3 evidence connections** that reveal deeper theories.
- **2 forensic puzzles**: rebuild the timeline and solve the Pre-Echo.
- **3 interactive call sequences** with response choices.
- **10 possible endings**, shaped by decisions, evidence and puzzle results.
- Local save progress on the device.

## Possible endings

THE SILENCE · THE LAST WITNESS · THE DOUBLE · THE LEAK · THE EXPOSURE · THE SOURCE · THE ORIGIN · THE REWRITE · THE PRE-ECHO · THE LOOP

Some endings depend on specific combinations of decisions, cross-references or puzzle results. You do not need to discover every ending in one playthrough.

## Important notes

- BLACK FILES is a fictional investigation game. The desktop, phone, notifications, calls, files, logs and forensic captures are simulated in-app elements.
- Incoming calls are interactive dialogue sequences inside the game; they are not real phone calls.
- The `mara_audio.msg` entry is represented through Mara's corresponding in-game message; it is not a playable audio recording.
- Forensic visuals are stylized in-game illustrations and interface graphics, not authentic surveillance photographs or real forensic captures.
- Progress is saved locally. There is currently no account system, email login or cloud synchronization, and progress does not automatically transfer between devices.
