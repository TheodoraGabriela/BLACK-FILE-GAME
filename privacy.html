BLACK FILES — Case 001: The Missing Signal

Effective date: November 19, 2026

English

BLACK FILES is a fictional, offline investigation game for Android. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information the app handles

The app does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. It has no account or sign-in system, advertising, analytics, or app server.

To save your case, achievements, and language choice, BLACK FILES stores game progress and preferences locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer or to third parties. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Permissions and third parties

The Android app does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or other sensitive device features. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. The game itself works offline.

If you open this policy link, it opens in your browser and loads this page from GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information about that website visit under its own privacy policy. That processing is separate from the game app.

Children

BLACK FILES is intended for players aged 16 and over. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For privacy questions, contact popa.theodora2001@gmail.com.

Aplicația nu colectează, transmite sau distribuie date cu caracter personal. Nu are conturi sau autentificare, reclame, analiză de utilizare ori server propriu.

Pentru a păstra dosarul, insignele și limba aleasă, BLACK FILES salvează progresul și preferințele local pe dispozitiv. Aceste informații nu sunt trimise creatorului sau unor terți. Le poți șterge din setările de stocare ale aplicației sau dezinstalând aplicația.
